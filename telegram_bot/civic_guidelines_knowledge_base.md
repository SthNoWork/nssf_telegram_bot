# Official NSSF & Cambodian Public Services Knowledge Base

## 1. NSSF Healthcare & Social Security Guidelines

### A. Contribution & Membership Rules
* **Formal Sector Workers (Employees):** 
  * *Eligibility:* The employer must pay contributions for at least **2 consecutive months** or **6 months within the last 12 months** for the member to access free healthcare at partner facilities.
  * *Post-Resignation Grace Period:* If a worker leaves their job, their NSSF card remains valid for **2 additional months** starting from the last declared contribution month.
* **Self-Employed Individuals (ស្វ័យនិយោជន៍):**
  * *Contribution:* 15,600 KHR per month.
  * *Eligibility:* Must have contributed at least 2 consecutive months to access full 24/7 care across partner hospitals. Can also register dependents (spouse under 60, children under 18) for the same monthly rate.

### B. Medical Coverage & Exclusions
* **What is Covered 100%:**
  * Outpatient & inpatient treatment at any NSSF-partnered public or private health facility.
  * Emergency care: Members can go to the nearest hospital (even non-partner) during emergencies and submit receipts for reimbursement via hotline `1286` or `1297`.
* **Public vs. Private Facility Rules (Chronic Diseases):**
  * *Chronic diseases* (such as diabetes, hypertension, heart failure, hepatitis B/C, kidney disease, and cancer) **are strictly covered only at Public Hospitals (មន្ទីរពេទ្យរដ្ឋ)** using essential medicines, not at private partner clinics.
* **Exclusions (Not Covered by NSSF):**
  * Routine dental cleaning, fillings, or extractions (unless tied to a specific workplace injury/illness).
  * Cosmetic surgery and elective procedures.
  * Artificial limbs/implants and fertility treatments not approved under specific workplace injury packages.

### C. Maternity & Cash Benefits
* **Maternity Cash Allowance (Royal Government):**
  * *Amount:* 400,000 KHR for 1 child, 800,000 KHR for twins, 1,200,000 KHR for triplets (plus additional personal contributions from leadership for triplets).
  * *Process:* Must register at an NSSF office within 3 months before delivery or up to 1 month after. Bring ultrasound/pregnancy book from public hospital, NSSF card, Khmer National ID, and an Acleda or Canadia Bank account.
* **70% Maternity Daily Allowance (Formal Sector):**
  * *Condition:* Must have contributed at least **9 months within the last 12 months** prior to delivery.
  * *Benefit:* 70% of average daily wages for the 90-day maternity leave period.
* **Self-Employed Maternity Benefit:**
  * Direct maternity allowance of **1,260,000 KHR** upon meeting contribution requirements.

## 2. General Public Service Guidelines (Future Roadmap / Expanded Scope)

### A. Driver’s License (Ministry of Public Works and Transport - MPWT)
* **New License / Renewal:**
  * *Locations:* MPWT service desks (e.g., Aeon Mall I & II in Phnom Penh) or provincial departments.
  * *Requirements (Locals):* Valid National ID card, physical health check certificate, and passing theory/practical tests (for new licenses).
  * *Requirements (Foreigners converting a foreign license):* Valid passport, valid visa, local residence certificate (certified by Sangkat), translated copy of home country license, and eye exam.

### B. National ID Card & Civil Registry (Ministry of Interior)
* **New ID / Renewal / Replacement (Lost ID):**
  * *Locations:* Local District/Khan administration office or central identification department.
  * *Requirements:* Family book or residence confirmation, old ID card (if renewing or damaged), and local police report (if lost).
